| Currency notes | Stage 60.60 is dated 2022-08-11. The open abstract says the standard specifies the process and criteria for decisions during an information exchange so that the project information model or the asset information model is of the required quality. It applies to delivery stages in ISO 19650-2 and to operational trigger events in ISO 19650-3. The abstract also explains the phrase “shall consider”: the person must think carefully about a listed item, and the depth of that thought depends on the asset, the people, and any national policy. No draft replacement was listed on the page opened on this date. UK BIM Framework Guidance 4 (March 2023) says a UK National Annex was published with the British adoption. That annex is out of scope beyond this mention. |

## Recommended reading order

1. P4-S01 (20 minutes). Read the abstract slowly. It is the only open ISO text for this part.
2. P4-S02 (75 minutes). Read sections 4, 5, and 7 with Table 2.
3. P4-S03, section 2.1 and section 5.3 (40 minutes). Use it to separate “shared” from “published”.
4. P4-S04 (10 minutes). Confirm you are looking at the current framework, then return to P4-S02.
