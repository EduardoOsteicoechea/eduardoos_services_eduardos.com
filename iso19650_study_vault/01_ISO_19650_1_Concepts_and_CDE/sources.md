## Rejected / link-only

- ISO 19650-1:2018 full text: paywalled-metadata-only. The catalogue abstract was used; clause text was not copied.
- Online Browsing Platform preview: fetch timed out. No term was taken from OBP.
- PAS 1192 and BS 1192: legacy (predecessor). Guidance Part 1 (July 2019) still points to PAS 1192-5 until ISO 19650-5 existed. That sentence is historical. Do not use PAS 1192 as the current rule set. [P1-S03]
- NHSScotland ISO 19650 Navigator: not used here. It mis-labels the publication years of Parts 1 and 2. See folder 05.

## Recommended reading order

1. P1-S01 (20 minutes). Read the abstract, stage, and “will be replaced by” line. Do not buy the standard for this first pass unless you already have a licensed copy.
2. P1-S02 (15 minutes). Note that edition 2 is not published. Study the 2018 concepts until it is.
3. P1-S03 (2 hours). Read sections 4 to 6, especially 5.2, 6.3, and 6.5. Treat section 3.3 on security as superseded by Part 5.
4. P1-S05, sections 1.2 to 1.4 (90 minutes). This is the clearest open account of the requirement cascade.
5. P1-S04, sections 1 and 2 (60 minutes). Read the state diagram and the metadata list before the UK status-code tables.
6. P1-S06 (10 minutes). Use it only to know where the guidance now lives.
