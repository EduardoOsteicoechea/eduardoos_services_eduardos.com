| Currency notes | Stage 60.60 is dated 2020-07-29. Review closed on 2024-06-04. The stage became 90.92 on 2024-07-17, showed 90.93 on 2024-09-17, and returned to 90.92 on 2024-11-07. The catalogue “will be replaced by” line lists both ISO/DIS 19650-2 and ISO/DIS 19650-3. Those drafts do not have the same scope. ISO/DIS 19650-2 (https://www.iso.org/standard/89704.html) is titled “Information management process” and its own page says it will replace ISO 19650-2:2018 and ISO 19650-3:2020, while its abstract still describes the delivery phase. ISO/DIS 19650-3 (https://www.iso.org/standard/90358.html) is edition 2, stage 40.93 on 2026-09-16, titled “Implementation of the information management process”. Its abstract says it gives guidelines for implementing the process in accordance with ISO 19650-2, and its page says it will replace ISO 19650-3:2020. Study the published 2020 standard. No amendment or corrigendum is listed on the life cycle opened on this date. |

## Rejected / link-only

- Full text of ISO 19650-3:2020: paywalled-metadata-only.
- OBP preview: fetch timed out.
- PAS 1192-3:2014: legacy (predecessor). The NHSScotland navigator still lists it beside the ISO series. Do not treat it as the current operational-phase standard. [P3-S01] [P3-S04]

- ISO/DIS 19650-2 abstract: not used as a description of the future operational process, because the abstract text and the “will replace” line do not tell the same story. Recorded only as a currency warning. [P3-S01]
