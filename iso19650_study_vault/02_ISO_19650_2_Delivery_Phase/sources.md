## Rejected / link-only

- Full text of ISO 19650-2:2018: paywalled-metadata-only.
- OBP preview: fetch timed out.

- CIC BIM Protocol, 2nd edition 2018: legacy (predecessor) of the UK BIM Framework information protocol. Use it only as history. [P2-S08]
- Commercial “free” BEP packs that require an email or an account (JES, Plannerly): not used. Access is gated, and they are not an official source.
- CIBSE DE3 (2016) and its templates: legacy (predecessor), written for PAS 1192-2:2013. Listed in folder 06 as historical context only.

## Recommended reading order

1. P2-S01 and P2-S02 (30 minutes). Learn the current edition and the revision warning before the process detail.
2. P2-S03, the activity map and clauses 5.1 to 5.4 (3 hours).
3. P2-S05 (90 minutes). Read the responsibility-matrix and TIDP/MIDP sections with clause 5.4 of P2-S03.
4. P2-S04 (75 minutes).
5. P2-S06, section 2 and Table 3 (45 minutes).
6. P2-S07 (45 minutes), then skim P2-S08 only as a template outline (30 minutes). Do not treat the template as a contract.
