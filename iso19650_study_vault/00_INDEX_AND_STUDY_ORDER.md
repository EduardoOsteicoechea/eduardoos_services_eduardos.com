# ISO 19650 study vault

This vault is a local study archive for the ISO 19650 series and the openBIM standards that sit next to it: level of information need, IFC, IDS, and bSDD. It was researched on 26 November 2026. It is written in English for an AEC professional who is also building C1 technical English.

Copyright stance. The ISO and CEN standards are paywalled. This vault records catalogue metadata and paraphrases open guidance. It does not copy ISO clause text. Quotations from open guidance are under 25 words. UK BIM Framework and NPSA PDFs are free to open, but their notices do not clearly allow a stored copy, so they are linked and not saved. The IDS 1.0 files in folder 06 were copied unmodified from the buildingSMART git tag under Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivatives 4.0. Do not edit those files.

## Out of scope, but encountered

- ISO 19650-6:2025, health and safety information, edition 1, published 2025-01, stage 60.60, 24 pages. https://www.iso.org/standard/82705.html
- UK National Annexes to Parts 2 and 4. One-line mentions only, inside the guidance notes.
- PAS 1192-2, PAS 1192-3, PAS 1192-5, BS 1192, and the CIC BIM Protocol. Legacy (predecessor). Not used as current rules.
- Vendor marketing and gated template packs.
