- UK BIM Framework guidance PDFs: link-only (licence unclear).
- JES ISO 19650 pack and Plannerly templates: rejected. Download is behind an email or an account.
- The Eng Hub and CIBSE files: link-only, and CIBSE DE3 is legacy (predecessor).
- Guidance Part B is too old to define IFC 4.3 or IDS 1.0. It remains a context source only.

## Recommended reading order

1. P6-S13, the level-of-information-need paragraphs in section 1.2 (40 minutes), then P6-S01 (20 minutes) and P6-S02 (15 minutes).
2. P6-S03 abstract only (20 minutes). Stop there; the draft is not published.
3. P6-S07 (30 minutes) and P6-S06 (20 minutes), then use P6-S08 as a reference site rather than a file to collect.
4. P6-S09 local readme and specifications note (90 minutes), then one facet note of your choice (40 minutes).
5. P6-S11 (40 minutes) and P6-S12 (30 minutes).
6. P6-S15 only after the ISO 19650-2 folder (30 minutes). Ignore P6-S16 until you want the pre-2018 history (20 minutes).
